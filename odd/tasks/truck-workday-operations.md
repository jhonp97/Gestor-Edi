# Truck workday operations

## Objective and context

Give the owner an accurate daily record of which worker drove which truck for which company, including a whole-day-idle replacement truck after a breakdown; keep one worker workday and one daily-rate snapshot. Surface those records in truck and worker workflows and produce comprehensible truck/company reports. The user's existing unrelated working-tree changes must be preserved.

## Scope and constraints

- One worker workday per civil date. Ordinarily one truck; a breakdown can involve two distinct trucks on that date. The replacement truck must have been unused for the entire date. Operational activity allocation defaults to 50/50 for two trucks, is editable, and totals 100%; one truck gets 100%.
- Record company worked for, per-truck distance when available, incidents, and existing actual truck expenses/income; do not infer company daily revenue, partner settlements, or duplicate worker wages.
- Preserve legacy daily-pay entries without invented historical truck or company associations. Existing PAID month guards, tenant isolation, historical amounts, and idempotence remain intact. Dates and money must be represented consistently.
- SonarQube, company revenue accounting, and partner settlement are explicitly out of scope.
